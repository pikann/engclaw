# Game content sources and attribution

`game-content.sqlite` is the read-only chapter catalog (SQLite, tables `chapters` and `examples`) of the pronunciation game (words, phrases and
idioms with IPA, a short meaning and example sentences). Its version and SHA-256 checksum are in
`game-content.manifest.json`. It is regenerated by `scripts/data/build-game-content.ts`; the
validation rules (no duplicates or empty fields, examples contain the target, length limits,
allowed licenses, at least 2,000 chapters) live in `packages/game/src/content.ts` and run in CI.

| Data                                                            | Source                                                                                                                                                             | License      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------ |
| Chapter texts, meanings, example sentences, per-record link     | [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/) contributors, extracted by [Wiktextract](https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract) via [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/) | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| Pronunciations (IPA, per word)                                  | [CMUdict](https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict), converted from ARPAbet                                                                                            | BSD-2-Clause |
| Frequency ranking (`frequencyRank`), derived from OpenSubtitles | [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) `en_50k` (2018)                                                                                     | CC BY-SA 4.0 |

Every record carries `source`, `license` and a `sourceUrl` pointing at the Wiktionary page its
meaning and examples come from.

## Filtering

Items are dropped when they are not in CMUdict (so IPA cannot be produced), when a word is outside
the 50k frequency list, or when Wiktionary marks the sense vulgar, offensive, derogatory, dated,
archaic, obsolete, dialectal or tied to a single region, or restricts it to a technical field. A
built-in blocklist removes profanity, sexual, violent and other sensitive vocabulary from texts,
meanings and examples. Duplicates and variants (for example a leading "to") are merged. Example
sentences must contain the target text as whole words.

## Difficulty, order and world

Each chapter has `difficulty` (0-100), a unique `ord` (easy-to-hard position) and a `world`
(contiguous runs of 75 chapters). They are computed from the catalog itself (frequency rank, IPA,
type) by `packages/game/src/difficulty.ts`; the per-phoneme error rates it uses are aggregates of
human ratings from speechocean762 (`scripts/data/extract-so762-errors.py`). CI recomputes the
ranking and compares it with the stored values. `scripts/data/rank-game-content.ts` re-ranks this
database without the Wiktextract dump.
